Privacy Policy — Lumibeat

Effective 19 November 2026 · Applies to the Lumibeat app on Android and iOS

Lumibeat is a daily-affirmations app. We built it to work without an account and to keep your personal data on your device. This page explains what little we do collect, why, and your choices.

1. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app's local storage on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • Your optional first name (used only to personalise reminder titles)
  • Your chosen topics, favourites, custom affirmations, reminder times, theme, language and text-size settings
  • Your streak (days you opened the app)

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. We have no servers that store your data and no way to access it.

2. Data processed by third-party services

Lumibeat uses two service providers. Each receives only what it needs to do its job.

ServicePurposeData
RevenueCatSubscriptions and restoring purchasesA random app-user id, purchase receipts from Google Play / the App Store, device type, app version. No name or email.
Google AdMobBanner ads in the free versionAdvertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, ad interaction data. Personalised ads only with your consent (see section 3).

Their policies: RevenueCat, Google.

3. Advertising and consent

  • In the EU/UK you are shown a consent form (Google UMP) before any ad loads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • On iOS, the system asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are non-personalised.
  • Subscribing to Lumibeat Premium removes ads entirely, and the ad SDK is no longer used.

4. Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push servers, so no reminder content or timing leaves your phone.

5. Sharing images

When you share an affirmation as an image, the image is created on your device and handed to the app you choose. We do not receive a copy.

6. Children

Lumibeat is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete; uninstalling the app clears everything local. For data held by RevenueCat or Google, you can contact them directly or write to us and we will help you route the request. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
